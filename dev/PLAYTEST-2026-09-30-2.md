## Driving the game: what changed

An attack order is reliable only as hover, look, then click: move the cursor onto the enemy, zoom to see its highlight and name label, and right-click as the first action of the next batch. A batch that clicks the title bar for focus and then right-clicks an enemy got `order: move` instead, three times. Quickbar slots answer to the number keys (`1` for Shield Bash) where a mouse click on the slot did nothing. The tester's memory file has the rest.
