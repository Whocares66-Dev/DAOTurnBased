## Findings, worst first

1. **A turn handed to the AI right after the mod ended a condition is wasted.** Fight F, round 3. 19:21:27.379 `Dog's condition has had its turns: ended`, `round 3: Dog's turn (party, yours)`, `0x000002df: ended knockdown`, `busy with a execute effect the gate did not see; letting it run`. 19:21:27.430, `settings reloaded` with `party = tactics`. No `Dog's turn is its AI's now`, no `turn_handed_over`. Three seconds later `Dog's turn ends: chose nothing`, `Dog (0x000002df) chose nothing: combat state 1, disabled flags 0x0, stun 0 paralyze 0 knockdown 0 daze 0 sleep 0, current command 0, 0 queued, light AI 0, custom AI 0`. The same switch 50 ms into an ordinary turn (fight G) handed over and the dog attacked. So the hand-over is lost while the ended effect's command is still running, and the AI is never poked.
2. **A knockdown during a creature's own turn costs it two turns.** The turn in hand ends at once (`turn ends: stunned`), with whatever action was left, and `1 of its turns lost` then takes the next turn too: the dog knocked down in round 1 next acted in round 3 (`ended knockdown`), the soldier knocked down in round 2 next acted in round 4. A 6 s knockdown is one round by the mod's own clock. Whether the turn it interrupts should count as the lost one is a design call; today the same knockdown costs one turn if it lands a moment before the turn and two if it lands a moment after.
3. **The hero can be held turn after turn.** With initiative 3 Aedan was last in the round and both archers acted just before him: held 19:21:24.509 (603), round 2 lost; held 19:21:35.175 (803), round 3 lost; first action in round 4. Each hold was one turn and each ran out by itself (`condition_expired`). This is play test 2's finding 6 from the other side, and belongs with it as a design question.
4. **Tactics to manual on an AI's turn leaves no trace** (check 6): the turn does become the player's, with the action already spent, but there is no log line and no event to say so.
5. **`turnbased.state.last_refusal` is always an AI's** (check 5).
6. **When the last enemies die outside anyone's attack, the mod waits for the engine.** With all four killed by `stage` at 19:23:54.122 on the dog's turn, the fight ended 2.8 s later as `the game ended combat`, not as `no enemy left standing`; in between the dog's turn stayed open with nothing to attack. Harmless here, and only a staged case.
7. **An archer's ability that ends in 22 ms.** 19:21:52.877 `Howe Archer: use ability (action, from script)`, 19:21:52.899 `use ability held, no action left`, `ability 803 of 0x000007d9 held for 2 of its user's turns`, turn over in 0.56 s and nobody pinned. The cooldown was charged for a shot that does not seem to have been fired; the same archer's 803 had been `ready again` at the start of that turn.

No crash, no crash log, no `holds it still`, no `did not take`.
