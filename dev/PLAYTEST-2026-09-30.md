## Follow-up (the build session, 2026-09-30 evening)

Each finding checked against the log copy and the code:

1. **The dog's pin: confirmed.** The ending (`RemoveEffectsByParameters` for stun, paralysis, sleep, root and knockdown) did not take: 19 ms after it the dog was still in the pin's command, and half a second later the status read found it held. The cause, found by DAOLib (`703bc92`): its invalid object was NWN's 0x7F000000 rather than DAO's 0xFFFFFFFF, so the removal's default creator, meant as "any", named an object that does not exist, and the handler (0x00673940) removed only effects that object had made: none. The held clock had nothing to do with it. The mod's own no-object follows (`src/core/Types.h`). Meanwhile the machine no longer charges a fresh turn for a hold it has just ended: it logs a warning and lets the hold run out on the game's clock, each turn lost while it lasts. The bridge logs what held the creature before and after the ending (`ended pin, and pin holds it still`).
2. **The archers: confirmed, cause open.** Every AI attack now logs its distance, the weapon's reach and the engine's line of sight (DAOLib `859f2db`), so the next fight says whether they had a shot.
3. **Deaths: confirmed, fixed.** `<name> falls` (or `is gone`, or `is up again`) at the status read that sees it.
4. **Portraits: half right.** The two not released were of creatures the game had destroyed by the fight's end: there was nothing left to release them from. Whether a destroyed creature's renderer goes with it is `dev/DAOLIB_NEEDS.md`'s memory question. The line now says how many were gone.
5. **`ai_turn_speed`: confirmed, fixed.** The machine's waits (settling, an AI's thinking and follow-up) counted real time; they count the world's time now, which runs at the speed the machine set. `SetSpeed` itself works (DAOLib saw a fight run twice as fast).
6. **Camera behind a door frame: noted.** DAOLib's `FirstHit` could test the view behind a creature before the camera swings there.
7. **Orders that queue nothing: a note**, as the report says.
8. **A second instance truncating the log: DAOLib's**, reported to it.
9. **The dog's pace: the doc was wrong.** The party's dog has an appearance of its own at 6.99 m/s (1.50, 13.5 m a turn); 1.07 is a wild mabari's. `dev/TESTING.md` says so now.
10. **The runtime dialog: mine.** A mutant made the debug test binary abort, and the debug CRT waited on a dialog. The tests report to stderr now (`tests/quiet_crt.cpp`).

The End button's word, which sat low in play, is centred on its letters now rather than its line.
