## A note on method, and a correction to play test 16

A click made at the very pixel where the mouse already rested, after the capture tool had handed the focus back to the game, is no order at all, preview or none: with the preview off, 15:20:40.724 `the player's order: move`, `the order queued no command`, and the next click at the same pixel a proper order. The game does not pick again until the mouse moves. From then on every click here was made after moving the mouse a few pixels, and none was lost.

Some of play test 16's lost clicks were of that kind (those repeated at one pixel across tool calls). The ones that stand, where the mouse had just been brought to the place: on `fd3ec74` four of four lost; on `91f96e9` two lost and three walked. The flipping reads of the cursor's ground there were measured apart from the clicks and stand as written.
