# Play test 25, 2026-10-02 night: the first fight with a turn a bar of time

One game on `rebalance-issue-1` `6c7edf5` (DAOLib `e9c65f2`), `turnbased.dll` of 02:02, at debug level, played with no hands by `tools/timeplay.py --scenario smoke` through Eluvian's bridge as `turn-based-agent`, the game claimed: 02:08:39 to 02:09:43. Lyna's `AutoSave_2`, loaded only; `[turns] clock = time` set for the run and put back to `actions` after; the game quit and the claim released. Transcript `build\timeplay-smoke.txt`, log `build\timeplay-smoke-turnbased.log` (432 lines). No warning, no error.

Lyna on her dagger (`EquipItem`) walks into the first hall, two giant spiders drop, and the party's turns are attacks on the nearest spider until the turn is over; the spiders are the game's AI.

## What was seen

1. **A swing costs the game's own time.** Lyna's dagger: `Lyna: attack costs 1.50 s` every time (one weapon's 2.0 s and a dagger's -0.5, her attack speed at rest), read through the script calls on the first try. The spiders, not humanoids: the stand-in, 2.00 s. Tamlen's longbow: the stand-in, 2.00 s.
2. **A walk to strike is paid with the swing.** Her first attack, from 6.0 m: `attack costs 1.50 s and its walk 0.58 s` (3.6 m to the reach of 2.4 m, a metre of it free, at 4.66 m/s), `1.92 s and 12.6 m left`.
3. **Several actions a turn, and the one that does not fit lands on the next.** Round 1: two swings (4.00, 1.92, 0.39 s left), the third `begins attack (1.50 s): 1.11 s of it is over what is left, and it lands on its next turn`, and her turn ends. Round 2: `a bar of 4.00 s, 1.11 s of it paid for its pending attack: 2.89 s and 14.5 m left`, `its kept attack on 0x305 is given to it`, the attack runs, and she is waited on with 2.89 s. Over three rounds she swings 4 s over 1.5 s a turn: 2, 3, 3. The same for the spiders: `Giant Spider begins attack (2.00 s): 0.58 s of it is over`, given again on its next turn.
4. **An AI with time left chooses again.** A spider in reach: two attacks a turn (`2.00 s`, `0.00 s and 1.0 m left`), its turn over as its bar is spent. One that has to walk pays as it goes (it left Lyna for Tamlen 8.9 m off mid-turn: `1.42 s and 9.0 m left`, then its next attack kept with 0.42 s left).
5. **The bar is spent to the millisecond**: Tamlen's two shots, `0.00 s and 1.0 m left`, `Tamlen's turn ends: nothing left to spend`. The free metre does not keep a spent turn open.
6. **A kept attack is dropped when its creature falls** (`Giant Spider's cast of ability -1 is interrupted: fell`) **and as the fight ends.**
7. Survival's rank is read: `Lyna (player): ... Combat Tactics 0, Survival 1`, and her initiative was 17 against the spiders' 16.
8. Pace: three rounds of four combatants in 50 s, about four seconds a turn of real time.

## Findings

1. **The words are the charge rule's.** A kept attack is logged and heard as a cast: `Lyna's charged cast of ability -1 is due`, `cast of ability -1 is interrupted`, the events `cast_charging` and `cast_landed` with `value` -1. To be an action's: its own events and lines.
2. **"Interrupted" floats over Lyna as the fight ends** with her attack pending (`Lyna's cast of ability -1 is interrupted: the fight over`). Nothing was interrupted: no floaty at the fight's end.
3. **"Next turn" floats on every turn of every attacker**, since an attacker's bar is rarely spent to the swing. On the player's own order it says what happened; on every enemy every round it is noise. To be the player's only, or shown on the card.
4. **No rollover was seen**: with an attack always begun, a bar is always spent. Rollover is what a turn ended early keeps; a scenario of its own.
5. **Bows and creatures are on stand-ins.** DAOLib has since read how the engine times each (its message of 02:10): a humanoid's melee swing is the script's formula, which the mod has; a creature's is its attack animation's length; a shot is a draw (0.8 s a follower, 1.5 s the player, 2.0 and 2.5 s in heavy armour), an aim loop and the release animation. `dao::animation::Length` and `AttackDuration` are written there and not yet committed.

## Not covered
