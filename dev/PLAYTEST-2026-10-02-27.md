What I would change or ask about:

- **"X s of Y s left" reads two ways.** "1.5 s of 4.0 s left" can be taken as "1.5 s are left of 4.0". "Costs 1.5 s (4.0 left)" or "1.5 s, leaving 2.5" cannot. The first time I read "2 s of 2.0 s left" I had to think.
- **The cost has one decimal or none by turns**: "2 s of 4.0 s left" for the bow, "1.5 s of 4.0 s left" for the dagger. "2.0 s" would match.
- **An ability waiting for its target is quoted the attack's price.** Lyna's Dirty Fighting (key 2, then the mouse on a spider): the caption said "1.5 s of 4.0 s left"; the log charged "use ability costs 2.00 s". And hovering the talent on the quickbar changes nothing in the caption (the game's own tooltip shows, with "Cooldown: 20s" in seconds).
- **The walk's price differs between an attack and a move.** Ground: 17.6 m for 3.6 s (4.9 m/s). Attack: 1.9 m of walk added 0.2 s, 4.4 m added 0.7 s, 6.2 m added 1.1 s. If that is meant (the walk overlapping the swing's start), fine; it looked inconsistent from outside.
- **The caption sits on what it describes when the target is high on the screen.** With the far spider under the cards the caption covered the spider's name label (`time-tamlen-hover-bow-cost`), and the "17.6 m" tag ran into the caption's last word (`time-lyna-hover-ground-walk-cost`). After the camera turned, Tamlen himself stood under the caption.
- During the walk-now order the idle caption read "0.0 s left, 4.6 m to walk": no time, yet metres to walk. True (the walk was paid for), but it reads as a contradiction.

## 5. Feel

By mouse I am slow (seconds of my own between every look and click), so for the clock I played fights 2 and 3 by orders given the instant a turn waited, which is the floor a fast player could reach.

- **Speed 1, two spiders, two party members: a round takes 16 to 17 s** (fight 2: rounds began 03:59:09, :22, :38, :55). A party attack takes about 2.1 s on the clock from order to the next order. A spider's turn takes 3.0 to 4.0 s. The fight: 5 rounds, 62 s from Lyna's second turn.
- **Speed 2, three spiders, two party members: a round takes about 10 s** (fight 3: 04:00:45, :56, 04:01:07, :17). Party attack 1.1 s, spider turn 1.4 to 1.8 s. 8 rounds in 74 s.
- **What drags at speed 1 is the enemies' turns in a row**: two spiders are 7 to 8 s of watching, and every spider turn has the same shape (land the owed bite, bite, begin another that lands next turn). With five or six enemies that is 20 s a round with nothing to do. At speed 2 it is 3 to 4 s, and I would rather play there, or with speed 2 for the enemies' turns only. I could not judge whether the animations look rushed at 2: I see stills, not motion. That part wants the user's eye.
- **One enemy turn took 10.7 s** (fight 2, round 5, 03:59:54.5 to 04:00:05.5): the spider landed its owed bite, then "use ability" at 03:59:55.7 with 0.39 s left, and nothing more until 04:00:04.97. Probably Overwhelm on Tamlen (his health fell 115 to 80 over the fight). A 9 s action inside a 4 s bar is the one moment the fight stopped feeling like turns.
- **Enemies' owed attacks make their turns free of choice**: every spider turn began "1.36 s of it paid for its pending attack" (or 1.61, 1.92). They always end their turn by starting a bite they cannot afford. It is the same rule as the player's, but it means their first act each turn is fixed and each of them bites three times in two turns. Not wrong; worth knowing for balance.
- The camera cuts to each actor at each turn; in fight 1 it turned right round between Lyna's turn and the spider's (after I had turned it with Eluvian's `camera face`, so that one may be mine).
- Tamlen's key 3 is Precise Striking, sustained: turning it on cost no time, as the ini says.
- Nothing surprised me in the rules themselves. Two bow shots or two dagger swings and a third that carries over is easy to plan with; the "to walk" figure shrinking as time is spent (19.6 m, 10.3 m after 2 s) was clear without explanation.

## What was made of it

The rules read clearly to a player who had hovered first, and nothing in them surprised: the brief's "like it was always there" holds for the bar and the captions. What is wrong is at the edges, and each is taken:

1. **"Out of movement" over a walk that got there** (4, 6): the walk to a kept attack's target is the mod's own order, a little longer than the metres paid for: it is to stop without a word, and have the slack it needs.
2. **The experience bar's flicker mid-fight** (1, 6): between two turns the view said the turn was not timed (the budget is reset as a turn ends), and the bar went back to experience for a third of a second. The view is to say so by the setting.
3. **The caption's words** (2): "Costs 1.5 s, leaving 2.5 s", always a decimal; nothing while a paid walk is under way; and at a turn that began by paying for a pending action, a word of why the bar starts short.
4. **"Next turn" lost among the floaties** (3): the pending action belongs on its creature's card and in the caption, where it stays, rather than in a floaty that the next turn's announcement lands on.
5. **An ability awaiting its target is quoted an attack's price** (2): the preview does not know an ability is being aimed. Asked of DAOLib: the ability explore mode is targeting.
6. **The refused attack logged 23 times in 0.7 s** (6): a companion's own AI trying at a spent turn; to be said once.
7. **A spider's Overwhelm ran 9.3 s for 2 s of its bar** (5): the open question of "What no rule reaches" (an action that is being held), now seen: the grab's own time is to be charged, what is over the bar owed to its next turns.
8. **Enemies' turns in a row are what drags** (5): 3 to 4 s each at speed 1, half that at `speed = 2`, and the tester would play at 2, or at 2 for enemies' turns only (`ai_turn_speed`). Whether 2 looks rushed is the user's eye's to say.
9. **The walk's price is the same for an attack and a move** (2): a metre of every turn's walking is free, so 1.9 m adds 0.2 s and 17.6 m costs 3.6 s. Told to the tester.
10. Every enemy turn beginning with an owed bite: the 2 s stand-in for a creature's attack; DAOLib's measured 1.5 s for a spider (0.81 of its attack animation) goes in next, and the pattern changes with it.
