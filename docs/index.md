# DAO Turn-Based

Turn-based combat for Dragon Age: Origins, Awakening and Witch Hunt. When a fight starts, everyone rolls initiative and acts one at a time. Talents, spells, enemies and difficulty are unchanged.

![A turn-based fight]({{ "/assets/img/fight.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

## Features

- **[Turns]({{ '/turns/' | relative_url }})**: each turn is 4 seconds to spend on attacks, abilities and movement. Unused time rolls over to the next turn.
- **[Portrait bar]({{ '/portrait-bar/' | relative_url }})**: everyone in the fight, in the order they act.
- **[Cost estimation]({{ '/portrait-bar/' | relative_url }}#cost-estimation)**: the time an attack or a move will cost, shown before you order it.
- **[Party control]({{ '/party/' | relative_url }})**: play each companion's turn yourself, or let its tactics play it.
- **[Options]({{ '/options/' | relative_url }})**: an on/off switch, fight speed and key bindings in the game's Options menu.

To install, see [Getting started]({{ '/getting-started/' | relative_url }}).
